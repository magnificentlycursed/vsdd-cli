The swarm-consumable projection of the remaining build program, derived from the contract's `## Decomposition (phase 1c)` section (`.design/agent-first-vsdd-toolkit.md`) under the ratified remediation design (the `decomposition-topology-remediation` knowledge page, REQ-1/REQ-2). ## Requirements

### Phase 2: Slice 2 — composition, generated context, and static price (sequential)

Provenance: Slice 2 — Composition, generated context, and static price

Depends on: the engine's data (composition-scope, economics-data, domain-prompt frontmatter — all present under `templates/registry/` and `.claude/commands/`). Entry decisions carried from the ratified slice design, resolved at this phase's 1b or by Solution Owner ruling: pricing-function home (a minimal cost crate now versus provisional in vsdd-core — recommendation: the crate, avoiding relocation of a mutation-covered pure function); the DESIGN.md surfaces reader and its closed vocabulary bound to the `activation_criteria` tokens (may route to the data-authoring package); the review-config loader shape (bespoke loader reusing `schema_check`/`bounded_read` primitives — recommendation — versus a registry-set class); and the token-count stamping method (which tokenizer, its determinism, stamp placement).

- **The composition function** (new `vsdd-core/src/composition/`, pure): maps (surfaces | fix-scale finding labels, composition scope, review config) → (active domain set, dispatch shape); one function at both scales; no token counts. Property tests: determinism and domain-narrowing; pair co-activation, pair separation, and validator-differs-from-owner hold in its output at set sizes 1, the `minimal` preset, and the full 18-domain set; the fix-scale fixture (finding labels under the `fix-lane` scope member) yields a deterministic active set.
- **The review-config loader** (`.vsdd/config.yaml`, net-new): layered loading (shipped presets seeded from `economics-data.md` → project config → local overrides) exposing active domains, round budget, stop sensitivity, model tiers and effort levels, mutation floor, cost bands; shape/schema-validated at read (the vsdd bootstrap leg) with bounded reads, value-free diagnostics, and no panic on malformed/absent/permission/oversize input.
- **The context generator** (new `vsdd-core/src/context/`): emits each dispatch's session-skill, domain-prompt, and supplement sections from the bundled sources, byte-matched to the (cleaned) source emission, with derived domain-to-supplement mappings, declared-absent distinguished from not-yet-authored, and a deterministic stamped token count on every artifact; wires `clean_for_terminal`/`clean_json_strings` at the external-sourced injection points (surfaces, config-derived names, finding labels) — the byte-match holds against the cleaned emission.
- **The token-budget gate and the pricing function**: the gate compares stamped counts against the per-class budgets in `economics-data.md`, warn at session start, block (non-zero) at the gate surface (CI wiring is Phase 6's); the pure pricing function emits the priced bill of materials (total = sum of parts, cacheable ≤ total, deterministic) at session start, before the spend.
- **The guardrail, three grades named**: the validation-at-read leg, the crosslink session-start hook (a thin wrapper invoking vsdd to compute the composition and emit the bill; logic in the tool, not the script), and the token-budget gate. The config-integrity RULES (pair co-activation, pair separation, validator-differs-from-owner rejection) are mdatron's family — a flagged cross-repo dependency gating on the release that ships it; the seeded integrity-violating configs are recorded as riding that dependency, never silently reported covered by vsdd.
- **The install payload's generated members** — the declared split's second half: once the generator lands, the generated skills and domain prompts join the `vsdd init` payload, and this payload-extending act re-runs the fresh-container install-and-smoke check over its payload delta (oracle: every installed-artifact-manifest entry resolves and `vsdd status` renders a working segment at exit 0). Provenance: Slice 3 — Install: packaging the guardrails for adopters

Obligations: closes **Composition purity** (the integrity-violating-config falsifiers ride the flagged mdatron dependency; the vsdd shape leg is live at landing); closes the generated-members split of **Install behaviors** (the static half is complete — see Completed phases); feeds **Cost queries** as a dependency, not a criterion. Owes at 2a: its red-gate seed, its fixture-corpus slice, and the operator-authored manual-test checklist; wires the terminal-output-safety cleaner into the generated-context surface as this phase's own act. Elaboration: knowledge page `composition-slice` (the retired ratified design; this phase is its projection).

### Phase 3: Slice 4 — gate execution and the mutation floor (sequential)

Provenance: Slice 4 — Gate execution and the mutation floor

Depends on: Phase 2's composition behavior (fix-scale labels; the config's mutation-floor activation field), Slice 3's install behavior (built), and the engine's data (`templates/registry/gate-data.md`). Extends Slice 1's shipped `vsdd gate` with subcommands (`vsdd gate red --scope layer|fix`, `vsdd gate phase-exit --phase 3|4`, `vsdd gate mutation` — ratified surface); the bare `vsdd gate` keeps its 0/1/2 contract unchanged.

- **The red-gate engine, checkout-and-run at both scales**: layer scale (2a closes only on a recorded failing run against the pre-implementation commit; 2b on the same suite green at HEAD); fix scale (baseline from the finding's recorded commit, the pin test APPLIED to the baseline, run in isolation, red at baseline and green at HEAD; the declared expected-kind set required, never inferred); the executed-pin discipline (built, collected, ran — skipped/ignored/filtered/not-collected satisfies neither half); wrong-reason by membership against the declared set; the flake policy (three runs per gate execution, aggregation at the declared-set grain, mixed outcomes satisfy neither half); results recorded as `GateResult` with the existing `GateKind` variants, written to state and tracker. Agent assertion plays no part.
- **The standing-suite delta and the three removal-shaped lanes**: no baseline-passing test may fail at HEAD; the removal-shaped set partitions into deletion / mapping (set-valued from→to pairs) / disablement, each clearing the single validator-approval bar via the git-tracked approval record (`.vsdd/removal-approvals.yaml`, ratified schema, #836) checked by set-equality — every computed removal declared, every declared claim computed; an undeclared vanished path fails closed.
- **The invocation stamp and the no-executed-pin forms**: `GateResult` gains `invocation: Option<InvocationStamp>` (nonce, wall-clock and monotonic basis, ordered run pair, runner; forward-compatible optional-with-default); split invocations and missing post-Slice-4 stamps fail closed. Graded retrofit (full record where expressible, validator-approved surface-limitation where not, partial declared partial — the deviation record stands; the fix still trips Phase 4's lifecycle falsifier). The three no-executed-pin forms — surface-limitation, declared compile-defect (its compile-red validly satisfies), broken-verification-surface — meet the validator-approval bar, with the gate's own baseline run attempt rejecting a false cannot-run claim (`cannot_run_predicate` in `gate-data.md`).
- **The phase-exit gates over fixtures**: phase-4 exit consumes the engine's `unrouted_findings`/`gate_verdict` unchanged (empty-query to pass; report and gate never diverge — one predicate); phase-3 exit is the terminal-round stop-signal check over acquired round children and dispositions; plus the state-consistency deep-history fixture (the concurrent-boundary clean-merge-inconsistent case reported, not silently accepted).
- **The mutation floor**: `cargo-mutants` CI-enforced on vsdd-core, `--in-diff` against the merge base; kill-ratio = caught / (caught + missed), unviable and timeout excluded; the floor NUMBER (65% starting calibration, ratified #836) is authored into `gate-data.md` as this phase's data-authoring item — the config declares only whether the criterion is in force.
- **Guardrail and fixtures**: extend `routing-gate.yml`/`vsdd-test.yml` with the gate legs (no new surface) plus a thin pre-commit wrapper running the fix-scale gate; wire `clean_for_terminal` into gate outputs; land the runnable-mini-repo fixture form (`vsdd-core/tests/fixtures/gate/`, tiny trees materialized into throwaway git repos so the gate really checks out and runs) beside the snapshot-based corpus, and author the fix-lane fixture corpus (~40 members: compliant-fix, green-at-baseline, skipped-pin, wrong-reason, spurious-compile-red, declared-compile-defect, pin-green-suite-red, rename-in-fix, the deletion/mapping/disablement lawful and unapproved forms, decoy-mapping, bulk-mapping-mismatch, in-place-quarantine, cfg-vanished pair, relocated-dormancy, split-invocation, both retrofit forms, both broken-surface directions, mutation-floor both sides).

Obligations: closes **Gate coverage**'s gate-mechanics members and its unrouted-findings phase-4-exit fixture (the round-parity/never-started fixture homes at Phase 5 — named split); closes the gate-mechanics primary slice of **Red-gate cheat blocked**; closes the state-consistency deep-history half of **Status detection**. Owes at 2a: the fix-lane red-gate seed and fixture-corpus slice, the operator-authored manual-test checklist, the per-commit guardrail wall-clock budget (Slices 1 and 4's standing obligation), and the fresh-container install-and-smoke re-run over its payload delta (every payload-extending slice's standing obligation). Elaboration: knowledge page `gate-execution-slice` (the retired ratified design; this phase is its projection).
