## The big picture

**1. Where this repository stands.** A reconciliation on 2026-10-08 found 39 decisions recorded somewhere the governing documents do not reflect, across all seven milestones; the operator had found two of them (the home of the session-start hook; the never-done crate collapse). The mechanism is four of this repository's own rules interacting: every directive is recorded at receipt (the contract's Directive reconciliation); the contract changes only through an owned cycle (Solution Owner change authority); recorded decisions freeze (The operator authors the oracle); and the ruling that the design folder holds only the contract and the build-plan, which retired the per-milestone designs to knowledge pages (`vsdd-cli#860`). Two more causes: the contract binds to crosslink's and mdatron's specifics in normative text, so every sibling move became a contract cycle; and a mid-flight re-scoping wrote no residual, so the crate collapse folded into the live self-governance milestone (`vsdd-cli#820`) vanished between two comments on 2026-07-29. Measured context: thirty commits since that milestone merged on 2026-08-02, none a feature; six design fixes in ten weeks, each repairing the artifact and leaving the recording rules unchanged; three active weeks of re-entry after a six-week break, not a design-to-build gap. Record: the pages "Reconciliation ledger (2026-10-08)" and "Append-accumulation retrospective (2026-10-08)".

**2. How the method's author practises it.** Four repositories of the organization that publishes the VSDD whitepaper were reconstructed read-only by four agents (Thermite, Peritus, OpenClaudia, crosslink). No repository uses the phase vocabulary. None has a red gate for feature increments; failing-first holds for bug fixes and for a critic's failing tests, and mutation testing substitutes. Review is one read-only reviewer plus continuous integration, with a written stop rule, and zero GitHub reviews across hundreds of pull requests. Termination is mechanical. Formal hardening comes first, not fifth. There is no refactor phase. Status wording keeps three evidence classes distinct (deterministic gates passed; independent review passed; a verifier's attestation recorded) and never upgrades one in prose. The design document is written just before its build and amended during it. Compared with this repository's contract: ours is stricter on process governance (a mechanized red gate for every increment; a multi-domain review of the specification and the code; proof from the trace that disciplines fired; dispatch ceremony); theirs is stricter on code-facing mechanical gates (a requirements registry with typed evidence; drift pinned to code; architecture as policy; a review ledger on the protected branch; anti-stub gates before an edit). Their controls are cheap per increment and were exercised; ours are expensive per act and were not built. Only one side shipped, and both paid: zero verified slices in OpenClaudia, a release policy never reached in Peritus, five dormant weeks of gates in Thermite. Record: the index page "VSDD in practice across the methodology author's repositories (2026-10-08)" and "Standard comparison: where this estate's process exceeds the reference repositories, and where it falls short (2026-10-08)".

**3. Document practice.** The attested shape is one umbrella document, frozen within days, plus one document per milestone written at build time against a named revision, decisions written at the question, amended as the code lands, pinned to the code it governs. Design-to-build latency ran hours to ten days in every repository. Thermite's RFC process document names our failure: "an issue is a report; amendments become comments, so the document a reader sees first is the stalest version of it." Record: "Reference practice: design documents, flow control, and this estate's divergences".

**4. Verification and the verdict record.** Two papers the Peritus maintainer pointed at on 2026-09-11. "Vero" (a repository-scale benchmark for verified software) shows an independent acceptor that re-renders submissions onto a pristine copy, rejects fourteen percent of residual failures as cheating, and lets an agent contest the benchmark itself with a machine-checked certificate, which found 34 of 38 defects that human review had passed; it also shows that reasoning effort is superlinear in yield only under an all-or-nothing metric. "Proof-carrying cognition" shows a reviewing model losing soundness as candidates rise (from 0.835 at two to 0.729 at thirty-two) and that selection alone manufactures a hacking gap from honest samples; it also reports its own failed predictions. Peritus's review ledger, where an approved record must already exist unchanged on the protected branch before a transition counts, is the first implemented form matching the evidence re-home direction recorded on `vsdd-cli#873`. Record: the two paper pages and "Cross-reference: the two verification papers, the Peritus maintainer's reading of them, and this estate's design (2026-10-08)".

**5. Agent-built test machinery.** The recursion.wtf article on property testing with agent swarms, and its skill: agents build reference models, generators of interacting operations, and assertions; the machinery finds the bugs (thirteen, four, two and more than thirty in repositories with thousands of existing tests). Each bug ships as a branch from main with only the regression test and the fix, failing before and passing after; the discovery suite stays elsewhere. That is this repository's fix-lane discipline, confirmed by an independent practitioner, and it gives phase 2a an attested form for new increments: machinery whose coverage is proved by removing an invariant and watching it fire. Record: "Property testing with agent swarms (recursion.wtf), read 2026-10-08".

**6. Orchestration shape.** ExoMonad, the recursion.wtf author's orchestrator: a tree of worktrees; a scaffold commit of types, failing tests, stubs and planning documents as the base every worker forks from (the only red gate for feature work any reference has, at the level of a parallel batch); no context reasons about more than about four children; recurring agent failures become hooks ("the guidance is encoded as code"), which is this repository's action-time-activation doctrine with the token cost made explicit. Tidepool adds suspension on an unresolved question as a resumable record, and budgeted truncation with continuation for serving large context. Record: the ExoMonad and Tidepool pages and "Cross-reference: the recursion.wtf articles against the 2026-10-08 findings and this estate's design".

**7. Prose and context.** Thermite binds a 3.5 KB register standard by one rule and carries it into every role. Palimpsest (a book-writing harness by the same maintainer) supplies protected intent, diagnosis separated from prescription, lint policies with density thresholds and intentional exceptions, and a corpus-level texture view. The clarity-review skill by the author of Observability Engineering, 2nd edition, was derived from about two hundred real review comments, carries a litmus test per pattern, and is never invoked by description match. The same author's memory rules add: capture confirmations, not only corrections; calibrate warning volume; precise falsifiable instructions; three instruction layers. The Context Language Models paper gives the formal statement of unintegrated decisions (append versus rewrite), the forgetting policy, and the immutable-log-beside-mutable-projection pairing this repository already has but did not use. The operator's complaint of 2026-10-01 about the agent memory system ("arbitrary persistence that is non-deterministic in when you do or don't abide by it") is the same problem at the memory layer; the deterministic ladder (hook, checked-in rules file, knowledge page attached to the issue, memory last) is the answer the three sources agree on. Record: "Reference practice: prose control", "The clarity-review skill", "Portable Claude Code memory rules", "Context Language Models", and "Cross-reference: context language models and model-managed context against the append problem and crosslink knowledge management (2026-10-08)".

**8. Knowledge management, provenance, feedback filed.** A split rule, six surfacing mechanisms, and a provenance form that supersedes rather than corrects are on "Knowledge-page conventions: a proposal (2026-10-08)". Eleven shape-tier capabilities observed in practice are filed as mdatron GitHub issue #79. Feature requests for crosslink's knowledge feature are listed on the context-management cross-reference page for the session that will handle them. Informal phrases used during the day are mapped to grounded terms, with source weighting (papers and standard lexicons; published authors; practitioners), on "Terminology grounding for the 2026-10-08 findings".

**What it adds up to.** One structural change and one behavioral one. Structural: restore a live, rewritable document per milestone beside the immutable log, written at build time with decisions inline, and move the bindings to crosslink's and mdatron's specifics out of the contract into data. Behavioral: build before amending, in increments the size of the live self-governance milestone, with one reviewer and a mechanical gold rather than breadth, and spend agent budget on test machinery. Everything else on the pages is hygiene that only matters once those two hold.

## The decisions

Each is the operator's under Solution Owner change authority. For each: why, which of the contract's goals it serves, the recommendation, the sources, and what to weigh.

### The scope of the conformance-and-efficiency member

*Why.* The contract member "Verifiable conformance and efficiency" is the largest member, zero percent built, every verdict could-not-check by its own rule, and the source of about a third of the 39 reconciliation items and of the most amendment cycles (`vsdd-cli#840`, `#869`, `#873`, `#876`, `#891`). Its evidence depends on a trace-sync channel that does not exist and was re-homed only on 2026-10-08.
*Goals served.* The member exists for the contract's law "authored is not exercised" and for the agent-first thesis; narrowing its build keeps both, because its cheap legs stay: the installed-artifact-integrity check (built) and the control-effectiveness fixture pairs.
*Recommendation.* Keep the member as the statement of intent; move its build legs (the trace-based verifier, the skill-invocation and operator-session audits, the spend-shape report) into a second program with its own umbrella after the composition, install, gate and lifecycle milestones ship. Keep in this program only what has a mechanical oracle today: the manifest check, the negative-case fixture pairs, and the dispatch manifest's recorded phase and dials.
*Sources.* The reconciliation ledger (the items on the conformance evidence's two homes, the operator-session audit's missing evidence, the report's provenance after the re-home, and the four expiring register entries); the OpenClaudia report (a verifier built as product features and never applied to a slice); the standard comparison page; Thermite's control-plane lesson (the smallest build that satisfies the law is a wiring check plus fixture-oracle tests).
*Weigh.* This decision removes the most append pressure and most changes what "done" means for this program. It is reversible: the member's text stays; the second program inherits it.

### The cadence: build before amending, in small increments

*Why.* The live self-governance milestone went from ratified design to shipped build in four days and is the only milestone that shipped. The composition and gate-execution milestones were ratified in late July 2026 and have not started; the three active weeks since the break were twenty-five governance commits and no feature commit. The Vero paper's cost signature (unfinished runs cost more than solves; a quarter of spend on the unsolvable) is what large all-or-nothing units produce.
*Goals served.* The contract's walking-skeleton doctrine and "enforcement live-early"; the per-milestone pull-request discipline assumes milestones small enough to open at phase 2a and close soon after.
*Recommendation.* A work-in-progress limit: while a ratified milestone design awaits its build, no amendment or tooling-adoption cycle opens unless it blocks that build; crosslink and mdatron releases land as pins and data. Split the composition milestone into two increments: first the composition function with its config loader and the state artifact; second the generator with its three outputs and the install members. Each increment starts from a scaffold commit (types, failing tests, stubs, the milestone's design document), the one attested red gate for feature work.
*Sources.* The retrospective page (the numbers); the four practice reports (design-to-build latency; Peritus's seven-day catalogue; ExoMonad's parallel batches and scaffold commit); the Vero paper's cost appendix; the property-testing skill's stop rule on budget.
*Weigh.* The limit will feel like refusing good ideas; the proposal documents under the rewrite decision take ideas without amending the contract.

### One owned amendment: live milestone documents, decisions in the document, sibling bindings in data, the hook passages

*Why.* The four interacting rules above, plus the retired integration home. The contract binds to crosslink swarm, the crosslink session-start hook, kickoff's container mode and the hub sync channel in normative text. Three passages still place this repository's guardrail hook inside crosslink's payload, which crosslink's hooks cannot carry (a project cannot ship hook scripts into crosslink's payload, and crosslink's init replaces a project's hook entries; verified by live test on `vsdd-cli#890`).
*Goals served.* Spec supremacy is kept: the contract becomes the frozen umbrella cited by section, as every reference keeps its thesis. "Conformance at action time" already says enforcement logic lives in pattern and registry data; this applies the same rule to vehicle bindings.
*Recommendation.* Reverse two rulings recorded on `vsdd-cli#860` in one cycle: the design folder holds the contract, the build-plan and one live document per open milestone; decisions are written at the question in the governing document, with the tracker comment as the log entry pointing at it (this reverses the contract's "provenance lives here, not in the sentences"). Move the swarm residue, the hook wording, the kickoff mode and the evidence channel into the paved-path map and the Claude Code rules file for the runtime. State the hook as this repository's own Claude Code entry, absent in kickoff worktrees by design. Stop squash-merging design pull requests, so each file's history is its version record. Carry the workspace sentence for the crate collapse in the same cycle.
*Sources.* The design-documents page (umbrella plus milestone documents in all four repositories; crosslink's design documents amended as status notes; Thermite's dated amendment paragraphs); Thermite's RFC process document; the content-delivery assessment and the Solution Owner reviewer's finding there that harness detail belongs in the runtime rules file and the paved-path map; the write-plans skill's lens on epistemic state and decision records; the Context Language Models paper's log-versus-projection framing.
*Weigh.* This is the one decision that is itself an amendment cycle; it should be the last cycle before the build window. It touches about six contract passages and the build-plan preamble.

### Routing the 39 reconciliation items

*Why.* They are promises with no payoff location; four register entries expire on 2026-11-29 and 2026-11-30 and a lapsed entry fails the deviations gate on every pull request.
*Recommendation.* Under the amendment above, each item becomes an inline resolution in its milestone document, with one routed comment on the owning issue citing the ledger page. Without that amendment, they become 39 tracker comments and the pattern repeats. The two date hazards get a re-arm now regardless.
*Sources.* The reconciliation ledger; Palimpsest's promise ledger, grounded as an obligations register on the terminology page.

### The review shape for this program's rounds

*Why.* The Solution Owner ruling of 2026-09-28 (recorded on `vsdd-cli#839`) makes the twelve-domain process-governing set mandatory for every review composition. The domain value scorecard's five datasets, the four reference repositories and the proof-carrying-cognition paper agree that breadth is not where value comes from, that a reviewing model loses soundness under selection pressure, and that late broad rounds manufacture findings.
*Goals served.* The contract's "Recorded review dispatch" asks for independence and a stop signal, not breadth; "the review budget follows the evidence" is already its text.
*Recommendation.* Not a request to reopen the ruling; the evidence is recorded so the operator can. If reopened: one read-only reviewer per round with typed findings, continuous integration as the second reviewer, a mechanical gold wherever one exists, a written stop rule, and the process-governing set as the audit-level expectation rather than the per-round dispatch.
*Sources.* The page "Domain value scorecard"; "Reference practice: roles, dispatch shape and review conduct"; the proof-carrying-cognition page; the clarity-review skill's pre-flight author pass as the cheaper form.
*Weigh.* Attended decisions freeze; reopening needs a change-classification record.

### The verdict record's form for the recorded-dispatch milestone

*Why.* Two rulings point at two homes: option (b), a signed trace on a hub branch, as an amendment in the gate-execution milestone (`vsdd-cli#876`, `#836`); and a dispatcher-written transcript digest signed by the operator, as the recorded-dispatch milestone's design-first act (`vsdd-cli#873`, 2026-10-08). Neither records the other.
*Recommendation.* Adopt the shape Peritus implements as the design direction: a content-addressed verdict record that must already exist unchanged on the protected branch before a transition counts, with an authorize-then-apply pair of pull requests so a source edit cannot approve its own review; no transcript sync. Record the supersession on both issues.
*Sources.* The Peritus report (its review ledger, actor registry and two-step pull requests); the verification cross-reference page; the reconciliation ledger's items on the two evidence-home rulings and the operator-session audit.

### The phase-skill rewrite, as its own issue

*Why.* The phase skills assume a program-wide contract, use a phase vocabulary no reference uses, and give phase 2a no attested form for feature increments. The reviewer roles for prose were written from principles; a published author derived the equivalent from two hundred review comments.
*Recommendation.* One issue, design-first through the design flow. Shape: grounded vocabulary where attested (increment, attestation, scaffold, freeze, gate), the phase names as the index; phase 1a producing one milestone document in the attested shape; phase 1c light (freeze, verdict, gap analysis); phase 2a split into failing-first for fixes and for critics' tests, plus machinery with a seeded-removal coverage proof for increments; phase 2b complete, not minimal; phase 3 as the review-shape decision; phase 5 continuous; phase 6 mechanical. Every skill carries a reader contract, intent tags on deliberate choices and a status, and is tested by fresh-reader calibration on a fixture. The Technical Writer and Documentation Reviewer roles derived from this repository's own review corpus with a litmus and a report form per pattern. A register standard bound by one rule and carried in every role. Ideas enter as proposal documents through the design flow, never as contract amendments.
*Sources.* The two phase-practice pages; the prose-control page; the clarity-review page; the memory-rules page; the plans-and-prompts skills page; the Context Language Models page (skills as evolvable texts scored on a held-out split); the terminology page.

### Records hygiene: knowledge conventions, the agent memory, two terms

*Recommendation.* Adopt the conventions proposal as convention: the split rule, the kind in the first line, the provenance form, supersession instead of corrections; split the four large legacy pages first; attach each milestone's input pages to its issue. For the agent memory, apply the ladder: move process rules out of the agent's memory into the project rules file, the contract or a hook; keep hard-to-rediscover facts and the why behind confirmed or corrected conduct; the agent writes nothing unprompted. Register "increment" and "attestation" when the phase-skill rewrite first needs them.
*Sources.* The conventions proposal page; the context-management cross-reference (the operator's complaint of 2026-10-01 and the three sources that answer it); the terminology page.
*Weigh.* Convention-grade until the knowledge branch can be walked by a checker; item eleven on mdatron GitHub issue #79 and the crosslink feature requests are the two sessions that would raise the grade.

### The crate collapse

*Why.* Folded into the live self-governance milestone on 2026-07-29 (`vsdd-cli#15` closed into `#820`), never done, no open issue; its only reason for a two-crate workspace, a separate cost crate, was retired (`vsdd-cli#870`).
*Recommendation.* Do it as the first increment's scaffold commit in the composition milestone, since that milestone adds three modules to the crate in question; carry the contract's workspace sentence in the amendment above.

**Two things to know.** The scope and cadence decisions remove most of the pressure that produced the pages; the rest are what the pages say to do with the pressure that remains. The only decision that costs an amendment cycle is the amendment itself, and it should be the last one before building; everything else is a ruling, an issue, a data change or a convention.
