# Feature: Decomposition-topology remediation and the deviation registry

## Summary

Remediate the hand-split decomposition topology (the parent contract plus the hand-split slice fleet — five independently-pipelined slice docs, plus a sixth sidecar on the #840 subsystem design and a built slice doc that never had one — a workaround crosslink cannot model) by converging on crosslink's intended one-doc-to-swarm flow through a derived build-plan document, and prevent the deviation class from recurring by making standing workarounds expire: a deviation registry — versioned data plus a `vsdd gate` leg. The control has two halves, graded honestly. The gate half EXPIRES registered deviations — mechanical once the gate leg lands. The registration half — that a deviation gets an entry at all — is forced only by the contract's stated-reason discipline (Conformance at action time: hand-rolling "carries a stated reason or is nonconformant") plus the review lenses: convention grade, with the unregistered-deviation residual named. The founding evidence proves the registration half alone is insufficient: the manual-dispatch fallback was recorded in the act-to-affordance map's condition field and calcified anyway — the calcification reading is the operator re-scope record on vsdd-cli #845 (2026-08-01); the contract's own affordance-map evidence line records that no crosslink workflow was ever self-summoned.

## Architecture

The build-plan doc is a `.design/` document like any other under the ratified Design-doc lifecycle member — authored via the design flow, sidecar written at birth (the sanctioned operation), amended under recorded dispositions. Its parser contract is crosslink's `parse_design_doc` (`crosslink/src/commands/design_doc.rs`): H1 title, known H2 sections, `requirement_groups` built only from `### Layer N:` / `### Phase N:` H3s inside `## Requirements` with `(parallel)`/`(sequential)` execution hints (`propose_phases_from_groups`, `crosslink/src/commands/swarm/init.rs:145-249`); everything else — including `## Completed phases` — lands in `unknown_sections` and is never grouped. That asymmetry is the mechanism this design leans on: one document carries the whole program's story while swarm sees only open work.

The deviation registry mirrors the shipped-registry pattern: template under `templates/registry/` beside `vocabulary.yaml`, `canonical-patterns.yaml`, and `anonymization-patterns.yaml`, deployed by `vsdd init`'s template plan (`vsdd-core/src/init.rs:722-770`) into `.vsdd/registry/` as a deploy-if-absent scaffold; the self-governance instance is tracked in-repo at the same path (Q1 as refined in Resolved questions). The gate leg extends the Slice-1 gate: `vsdd/src/main.rs` dispatches `Command::Gate` to `cmd_gate` (main.rs:205), which consumes `vsdd_core::answer::integrity`; the deviations check joins as a sibling verdict source reading the registry file, pure over the parsed data (the vsdd-core purity pattern), with the date comparison against a caller-supplied today (no clock in core). Machine-decidable retest triggers are evaluated where the evidence is reachable: date triggers in the gate itself; upstream-issue triggers in the CI leg (the strictness-flag mode with a provisioned `GH_TOKEN`, able to query GitHub), reported as inconclusive-not-pass when unreachable, per the preflight three-valued precedent and Q3; an unreadable or shape-invalid registry is fail-closed Unverifiable in both modes.

The slice-doc retirement reuses the #826 mechanics: absorb, `crosslink knowledge add` per doc, delete, with every citer repointed (the reference-sweep lesson: the sweep includes `.claude/commands/` primers, templates, and `*.rs` doc-comments; the contract is excluded — its repoints ride the amendment; mdatron's cross-reference check covers frontmatter slugs only, not body links). The contract amendment is deliberately the last act, and small: this design carries the substance; the amendment registers the control, re-points the Decomposition at the build-plan, updates the affordance map's swarm-entry binding, repoints the retired-file citations, adds the retirement-under-disposition clause, and reconciles the stated-reason rule and trust-boundary surfaces — each a named, reviewable clause (REQ-7 clauses (a)-(f)) under the amendment discipline this repo just exercised end-to-end on #826.

Exit notes: the crosslink knowledge page for this design is refreshed at ratification — a stale pre-resolution twin exists; and founding entry 3's `disposition_ref` is written at cycle exit — the ratification decision comment's issue number plus timestamp, with the closing boundary commit as that comment's cited evidence (AC-4 evaluates it there).
