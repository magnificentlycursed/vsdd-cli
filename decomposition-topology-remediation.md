## Design Specification

### Summary

Remediate the hand-split decomposition topology (the parent contract plus six independently-pipelined slice docs — a workaround crosslink cannot model) by converging on crosslink's intended one-doc-to-swarm flow through a derived build-plan document, and prevent the deviation class from recurring by making standing workarounds expire: a deviation registry — versioned data plus a `vsdd gate` leg — so that every off-affordance act or persisted bootstrap fallback carries a stated reason, a retest trigger, and an expiry, checked loud. Founding evidence: the manual-dispatch fallback the contract said "must not calcify" calcified undetected (vsdd-cli #845, operator re-scope 2026-08-01); the contract's own affordance-map evidence line records that no crosslink workflow was ever self-summoned.

### Requirements

- REQ-1: **The build-plan document.** One new doc, `.design/build-plan.md`, is the swarm-consumable projection of the remaining build program. Open phases appear as `### Phase N: <name> (parallel)` or `(sequential)` H3 headers inside `## Requirements` — the only surface crosslink's parser groups (`crosslink/src/commands/design_doc.rs:159-165` at 6b4f736f; H3-under-Requirements per the doc/code mismatch tracked as vsdd-cli #847 / upstream #57). Completed program history lives in a `## Completed phases` section outside `## Requirements`, which the parser treats as an unknown section and never groups — so `swarm init` proposes exactly the open phases and no skip-completed mechanism is needed. Every phase absorbs its content from the retiring slice doc (or, for Slices 5-7, is authored fresh from the contract's Decomposition member) and states its dependencies in the phase ordering hints.
- REQ-2: **Contract-tie discipline.** The build-plan is subordinate to the contract (`.design/agent-first-vsdd-toolkit.md`): contract-over-derived precedence, the same rule as contract-over-primer (Conformance at action time). Every phase carries a provenance line naming the contract Decomposition member it projects. The build-plan amends only under the Design-doc lifecycle member's rules (recorded Solution Owner disposition; no spec-type exception). Generation of the build-plan from the contract's Decomposition by Slice 2's generated-context machinery is a named future; hand-authored now under this discipline.
- REQ-3: **Slice-doc retirement.** The five slice pipeline sidecars are deleted (`composition-slice`, `finding-query-join`, `gate-execution-slice`, `install-slice`, `routing-before-fix-guardrail` — each `stage: designed`, `plans: []`, `runs: []`, never kicked off; verified 2026-08-01). The six slice docs (those five plus `terminal-output-safety.md`, which never had a sidecar) are absorbed into the build-plan — built work annotated complete in `## Completed phases`, open work as phase content — then retired: stored as crosslink knowledge pages (the re-sequence-enforcement-spine precedent) and deleted from `.design/` (git history is the archive, the #826 precedent). Retirement follows the ratified Design-doc lifecycle rules — the sidecar deletions are modifications of existing sidecars and land under this design's recorded Solution Owner disposition (the sanctioned disposition-carrying operation, not a freelance edit).
- REQ-4: **The deviation-registry data set.** A versioned registry beside the act-to-affordance map's data-set family (Conformance at action time names the map as AI-Engineer-owned versioned data): each entry carries `id` (plain name, no coined letter-clusters), `deviation` (what rides off-affordance or persists as a fallback), `stated_reason`, `retest_trigger` (a predicate — machine-decidable where possible: a date, an upstream issue state, a mirror commit), `expiry` (a date), `owning_issue`, `status` (`standing` / `re-evaluated` / `resolved`), and `disposition_ref` (the tracker comment recording the latest evaluation). Founding entries: (1) the manual-dispatch fallback — runtime-harness Agent/Workflow dispatch in place of crosslink kickoff/swarm; stated reason: upstream kickoff stall and missing injection seam (vsdd-cli #837/#851; upstream #55/#62); retest trigger: upstream #55 closed or the local mirror pulled past a fix; (2) the mdatron unpinned-sibling-checkout hazard already recorded prose-only in the contract's Estate cleanup member, with its written retest trigger (the sibling's default branch advancing past 0.1.x) — the registry gives that existing paragraph its fire-check; (3) the hand-split slice topology itself, entered as `resolved` by this cycle — the founding exemplar showing an entry's full lifecycle.
- REQ-5: **Registry homes.** The adopter template ships at `templates/registry/deviation-registry.yaml` (deployed by `vsdd init`'s template machinery, Slice 3 / `vsdd-core/src/init.rs`); the self-governance instance lives per Open Question Q1. The registry is YAML data, so its shape check is vsdd's, not mdatron's, per the tier rule's three-question boundary procedure (subject not markdown → vsdd implements).
- REQ-6: **The gate leg.** `vsdd gate` (the Slice 1 guardrail surface — `vsdd/src/main.rs::cmd_gate` → `vsdd_core::answer::integrity::gate_verdict`) gains a deviations check: enumerate the registry; fail loud on a `standing` entry whose expiry has lapsed, or whose machine-decidable retest trigger fires, without a newer `disposition_ref`; result semantics per Open Question Q3. The CI backstop follows the routing-gate.yml pattern (a required-check leg reading the self-governance instance). Grade honestly: convention at this design's ratification; CI-backed block when the leg lands — the leg is a build-plan phase deliverable homed with the Slice-1 guardrail family.
- REQ-7: **The contract amendment (follow-on, own commit).** After this design ratifies, one contract amendment under the Solution Owner change authority discipline and the Gates amendment format-carry: (a) register the deviation-registry control in Conformance at action time beside the act-to-affordance map, with its honest grade; (b) a Decomposition note pointing the remaining build program at the build-plan doc (the slice members stay as the governing capability statements; the build-plan is their projection); (c) the act-to-affordance map's swarm-entry binding updated — the build-plan's first `swarm init` is the Swarm live fire criterion's instrument.
- REQ-8: **Swarm entry and its gating.** The build-plan's first `crosslink swarm init --doc .design/build-plan.md` is the swarm live fire (contract criterion). It is gated on upstream: the kickoff-plan stall (vsdd-cli #837 / upstream #55) rides the same agent-launch infra. Until the gate clears, the manual-dispatch fallback continues under its registry entry — now with a retest trigger and an expiry instead of indefinitely. The vsdd-layer injection into swarm-dispatched agents depends on the template seam follow-up (upstream #62); the registry entry's re-evaluation cites both.

### Architecture

The build-plan doc is a `.design/` document like any other under the ratified Design-doc lifecycle member — authored via the design flow, sidecar written at birth (the sanctioned operation), amended under recorded dispositions. Its parser contract is crosslink's `parse_design_doc` (`crosslink/src/commands/design_doc.rs`): H1 title, known H2 sections, `requirement_groups` built only from `### Layer N:` / `### Phase N:` H3s inside `## Requirements` with `(parallel)`/`(sequential)` execution hints (`propose_phases_from_groups`, `crosslink/src/commands/swarm/init.rs:145-249`); everything else — including `## Completed phases` — lands in `unknown_sections` and is never grouped. That asymmetry is the mechanism this design leans on: one document carries the whole program's story while swarm sees only open work.

The deviation registry mirrors the shipped-registry pattern: template under `templates/registry/` beside `vocabulary.yaml`, `canonical-patterns.yaml`, and `anonymization-patterns.yaml`, deployed by `vsdd init`'s template plan (`vsdd-core/src/init.rs`); the self-governance instance is tracked in-repo (home per Q1). The gate leg extends the Slice-1 gate: `vsdd/src/main.rs` dispatches `Command::Gate` to `cmd_gate` (main.rs:205), which consumes `vsdd_core::answer::integrity`; the deviations check joins as a sibling verdict source reading the registry file, pure over the parsed data (the vsdd-core purity pattern), with the date comparison against a caller-supplied today (no clock in core). Machine-decidable retest triggers are evaluated where the evidence is reachable: date triggers in the gate itself; upstream-issue triggers in the CI leg (which can query GitHub), reported as inconclusive-not-pass when unreachable, per the preflight three-valued precedent and Q3.

The slice-doc retirement reuses the #826 mechanics: absorb, `crosslink knowledge add` per doc, delete, with every citer repointed (the reference-sweep lesson: the sweep includes `.claude/commands/` primers, templates, and the contract itself — mdatron's cross-reference check covers frontmatter slugs only, not body links). The contract amendment is deliberately the last act, and small: this design carries the substance; the amendment registers the control, re-points the Decomposition at the build-plan, and updates the affordance map — each a named, reviewable clause under the amendment discipline this repo just exercised end-to-end on #826.
